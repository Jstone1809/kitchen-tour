Walk around & open cupboards

Phone: left joystick to walk, drag anywhere else to look, tap a door, drawer or appliance to open / close it.
 Computer: W A S D or the arrow keys to walk, drag with the mouse to look, click a door, drawer or appliance to open / close it.
 The corner units (B7, B11) swing their LeMans trays out. Open all opens everything at once; the buttons jump to the hall door, the dining end or the sink / hob.
Built from the Blender model (fitter-pack sizes, room 5540 x 2780, ceiling 2210) with baked Cycles lighting. The drawings govern.
